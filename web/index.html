Personal use only

Tuned in Markets runs on your own computer, for you. Do not sell it, host it for others, or republish its data or screens. The prices and filings come from the sources below straight to your machine. They are not ours to license, and each source has its own terms that you agree to follow by using it.

Not financial advice

Everything here is information for your own research. It is not investment, legal or tax advice, and it is not a recommendation to buy or sell anything. Scores, signals, alerts, backtests and AI reports are mechanical outputs and can be wrong.

You are responsible for your trades

Any order you place is your decision and your risk. You can lose money, including all of it. Check prices and facts with your broker and with primary sources before you act.

Timing and accuracy
  • Live prices refresh every 3 to 15 seconds. US prices typically trail the exchange by about 10 to 20 seconds and Indian stocks by a few seconds. Some indices, such as the Sensex, are 15 minutes late. Do not use any of it to time an entry to the second.
  • Fundamentals, filings, scores and screens are rebuilt daily or weekly. They can be incomplete, restated or out of date.
  • Backtests are simulated trades with assumed costs on past data. They do not predict the future.
Where the data comes from
  • Yahoo Finance: prices, charts, daily history
  • SEC EDGAR: US company filings and insider forms
  • NSE India: flows, options, bulk deals, holidays
  • Nasdaq: index membership lists
  • Google News: headlines

The app paces its own requests. If a source blocks you or changes its rules, that part of the app stops working until it is updated.

Your contact for SEC requests

The SEC asks everyone who fetches its data by program to say who they are. Enter your own name and email. They stay in the .env file on your computer and are sent only to the SEC, in the request header it asks for.

AI research (optional)

Deep research uses your own API key from OpenAI or another provider. You pay that provider directly, a few cents to about fifteen cents per stock. Your key stays in .env and is sent only to that provider. The report is written by AI, can contain mistakes, and is not advice.
